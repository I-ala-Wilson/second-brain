# Module: people
Folder: lives in `01-profile/`

## Follow-up questions
- Important people (family, friends, mentors, colleagues) and what they help with.
- Birthdays as `MM-DD Name` (or later).

## Files
- `01-profile/people.md`: who, relationship, how to reach them (only what they share), what to ask them about.
- `01-profile/birthdays.md` from `kit/templates/birthdays.md`, one line per birthday.

## Index rows
| Who someone is, who to ask about what | `01-profile/people.md` |

Set `"hooks.birthdays": true` in `brain.config.json` if they want reminders.
