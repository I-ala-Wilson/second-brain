# Module: money
Folder: `08-money/`

## Follow-up questions
- Income sources, monthly budget, big expenses coming.
- Scholarships or bursaries they hold (and renewal conditions) or want to apply for.
- Banks and accounts by name only (never numbers or passwords).
- Money rules they live by (e.g. no debt, savings target).

## Files
- `08-money/budget.md`: income, fixed costs, budget, savings goals, money rules.
- `08-money/scholarships.md`: held awards with renewal rules, and a table of awards to apply for (name, amount, deadline, eligibility, link, status). Verify every new deadline and link with a web search.

## Index rows
| Budget, income, money rules | `08-money/budget.md` |
| Scholarships held and to apply for | `08-money/scholarships.md` |
