# Module: fitness
Folder: `06-fitness/`

## Follow-up questions
- Goal (strength, muscle, fat loss, endurance, sport), current level, injuries or limits.
- Days per week, session length, gym equipment available, exercises they hate.
- Running or cardio goals with current paces.
- Nutrition targets if they want them (protein, calories), dietary restrictions.

## Files
- `06-fitness/plan.md`: weekly split, exercises with sets/reps, progression rule, cardio plan, nutrition targets, preferences.
- `06-fitness/log.md`: dated entries for sessions, bests and body metrics.
